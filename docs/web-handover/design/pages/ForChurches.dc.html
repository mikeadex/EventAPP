Features

Everything from a Sunday RSVP to a sold-out conference

  • Create an event in minutes Add a cover photo, date, venue and description — your event page is ready to share.
  • Free RSVPs or ticket tiers Early bird, standard, group or concession tiers with their own limits.
  • Tables and seating Let guests reserve a whole table for galas, dinners and conferences.
  • Donations at checkout Invite an optional gift with every booking, or run a donation-only event.
  • QR check-in Scan tickets at the door from any phone. One scan per ticket, no duplicates.
  • Attendee lists See who’s coming, search by name and export your list when you need it.
  • Live sales dashboard Track bookings, revenue and check-ins as they happen.
  • Payouts to your account Ticket income goes to your church’s bank account, with clear payout summaries.
